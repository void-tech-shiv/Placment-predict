Outlier Detection & Handling

Extreme outliers can warp decision boundaries during ML training. Apply Interquartile Range (IQR) clipping to cap values outside $[Q1 - 1.5 \times IQR, Q3 + 1.5 \times IQR]$.

Clipping maintains dataset row count while capping extreme noise points safely.
Why Clean Outliers?
  • Prevents extreme CGPA or test score typos from biasing predictions.
  • Improves convergence speed for Logistic Regression and Neural Net models.
  • Increases cross-validation score stability.
